# Mudcrown FAQ

## The game

**Is it free?** Yes. No adverts, nothing to buy.

**Do I need an account?** Not to play. An offline career lives on your device. Signing in will
only be needed for online careers, the leaderboard and the transfer market, once they arrive.

**Where is my career saved?** In your browser's storage on the device you play on. Clearing the
browser's site data erases it, and it does not follow you to another device.

**Why can I not start in the First Division?** That is the game: every career starts at the
bottom of the Fourth Division and climbs.

**Are the clubs and players real?** No. Every club, player, town and competition is invented,
and a check in the game keeps real names out. The countries are real.

**How long is a season?** Twelve clubs in every division, each meeting every other twice: 22
rounds. The top two go up, the bottom two go down.

## Playing online (coming)

**Can I play against my friends?** Not directly. Every manager plays alone; online careers share
a leaderboard and a transfer market.

**Why can an offline career not go on the leaderboard?** A score worked out on your own device
could be changed, so only careers the server plays can be ranked.

**How old must I be to play online?** Sixteen or older.
