# Release notes

## 0.0.1, 27 September 2026

The first playable build, in the browser.

- Choose a manager name, one of eight countries and a club in the Fourth Division.
- Play a full season of 22 rounds, round by round, with the league table as you go.
- At the end of the season two clubs go up and two go down from every division, and a new
  season begins; your seasons are listed with the score each earned.
- Privacy and Terms pages.
