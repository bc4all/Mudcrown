# Mudcrown

**From park pitch to top flight.** A short, simple football manager in the spirit of the 1990s
classics. Free, no adverts, nothing to buy; playing offline needs no account.

**Play it in the browser: [mudcrown.com](https://mudcrown.com)**
(an early build). An Android app follows.

Pick one of eight countries, take over a park club in a local league at level 8, and take it up
eight levels to the top. Pick the team and the tactics, train the players, buy and sell, build the
ground and keep the owner and the fans happy; every season the top clubs go up and the bottom ones
go down. Every club, player, town and competition in the game is invented.

Played alone. Signing in keeps your careers in your account, so you can start on your phone and
carry on in the browser on your laptop; the game plays offline and catches up when you are back
online.

## What this repository is for

This is the public front door of the game: **bug reports, ideas and questions**. The game's
source and its licensed art live in a private repository, so there is no code here: this page,
the guide and its pictures, the FAQ, the release notes, the credits and the issue forms.

- **New to the game?** Read [How to play](HOW_TO_PLAY.md).
- **Have a question?** Read the [FAQ](FAQ.md) first.
- **Found a bug?** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/bc4all/Mudcrown/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Have an idea?** Open an [idea](https://github.com/bc4all/Mudcrown/issues/new?template=idea.yml).
- **Something else?** Open a [blank issue](https://github.com/bc4all/Mudcrown/issues/new).

Before opening one, look through the [open issues](https://github.com/bc4all/Mudcrown/issues) in
case it is already there; a thumbs-up on an existing issue counts.

### What makes a good bug report

- What you did, step by step, and what you expected to happen.
- What happened instead. A screenshot helps a great deal.
- Where you were: the screen, your country, division and season.
- Whether the career is offline or online.
- The browser and device (for example "Chrome on Windows 11" or "Firefox on a Pixel 8").

### What makes a good idea

- The problem it solves or the moment it improves, before the solution.
- Mudcrown means to stay short and simple. An idea that adds depth without adding screens is the
  easiest to take.

## Good to know

- Mudcrown is an early build. An offline career is saved in the browser, on the device you play
  on. Saves may be reset between versions while the game is young; if that happens, it will be
  said on this page.
- The game has no ads and no tracking.
- The game speaks English; Polish, Spanish and Italian are planned.
- No real league, club or player is in the game, and none will be. If a generated name happens to
  match a real club or person, open an issue and it will be changed.
