# Mudcrown FAQ

The same answers as the game's own FAQ (title screen, **FAQ**).

## The game

**Is it free?** Yes. No adverts and nothing to buy.

**Do I need an account?** No. Your career is saved on the device you play on. Signing in (Google, Microsoft or GitHub) keeps a copy in your account, so you can start on your phone and carry on in the browser on your laptop.

**Where is my career saved?** On this device: in the browser's storage on the web, in the app's data on a phone. It is saved as you play. Clearing the browser's site data or the app's data erases it there; if you are signed in, your account keeps a copy.

**Can I have more than one career?** One career at a time on a device, and up to three in your account if you sign in. To start again, press Delete beside it on the title screen; the game asks once more before it is gone.

**Are the clubs and players real?** No. Every club, player, town and competition is invented, and a check keeps real names out. The countries are real.

**Why can I not start higher up?** That is the game: every career starts in a local league at level 8 and climbs, from park pitch to top flight.

**Does it work without a connection?** Yes, always. Signed in, a career played in flight mode goes to your account when the device is back online.

## Playing

**Why did Continue not play the match?** Continue moves the calendar on day by day to the next match, and stops when a letter needs an answer. Press it again to go on.

**A player left for nothing. Why?** His contract ran out. Contracts are never renewed for you: offer a new one before it ends (Contracts, under the Market tab, lists those ending).

**Why can I not sign anyone?** Transfers are open in July and August and in January, three signings a window; free agents can be signed at any time. The fee must also fit the owner's transfer budget.

**Why are a player's ratings a range?** You have not scouted him yet. Send a scout (Scouting, under the Market tab) to see his true ratings.

**What does an orange rating mean?** The player is out of his position in that slot and plays below himself there.

**My team made changes I did not ask for.** That is the Game plan. It changes tactics and makes substitutions for you; any change of your own in the match turns it off.

**We were promoted and then sent back down.** The ground fell short of the new level's rules by the end of the first season up. The Desk lists what is missing; the Ground page builds it.

**How do I avoid the sack?** Meet the owner's target (Owner, under the Club tab) and keep the club out of the red. A missed target brings a warning letter; an ultimatum comes before the sack.

**Something is wrong or unclear.** Please tell us: the title screen's Report a bug or an idea opens the game's public issue list.

## Playing on more than one device

**How do I carry on on another device?** Sign in on the title screen on both. Your career goes to your account as you play, and the Online box on the other device lists your careers to continue.

**What if I played on two devices without a connection?** The next time one of them is online, the game shows both (season, date, when and where last played) and asks which to keep.

**How old must I be to sign in?** Sixteen or older. Anyone can play without signing in.

**Can I play against my friends?** No. Mudcrown is a game for one: no leaderboard, no matches or transfers between players.
